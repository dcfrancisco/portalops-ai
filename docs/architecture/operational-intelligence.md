# Operational Intelligence Philosophy

PortalOps is an Operational Intelligence Platform for Liferay.

PortalOps treats every assistant request as an operational investigation.

## What PortalOps Optimizes For

PortalOps optimizes for:

- Findings
- Risk identification
- Governance visibility
- Operational investigations
- Recommended actions
- Navigation to operational workflows

PortalOps does not optimize for:

- Chat conversations
- Long conversational threads
- Generic chatbot experiences

## Investigation Examples

Examples of PortalOps operational investigations:

- Analyze portal health
- Investigate search failures
- Review permission risks
- Analyze stale content
- Explain failed workflows

## Primary Questions

PortalOps should answer these questions first:

1. What did I find?
2. Why does it matter?
3. What should I do next?

Detailed narrative explanation comes after those answers, not before them.

## Operational Response Shape

PortalOps should respond with:

1. Summary
2. Findings Cards
3. Recommendations
4. Actions

This response shape reflects how operations engineers consume information:

- Identify the problem
- Understand the impact
- Move to action

## Product Implication

PortalOps cards are reusable operational intelligence components.

They can appear on:

- Overview
- Assistant
- Reports
- Notifications

PortalOps modules remain first-class destinations for operational work. The Assistant helps users investigate and navigate; it does not replace the rest of the product.
